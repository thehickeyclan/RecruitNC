## 8a. Coaches & Officials Lounge

**Principle:** One credentialed lounge — college coaches, credentialed high school coaches, and tournament officials together. Credentials required; not open to general spectators.

**Hope Church angle:** One of Hope Apex's five atrium rooms becomes the Coaches & Officials Lounge. Remaining atrium rooms support NC United ops, registration, and credentials.

### Combined lounge (larger atrium room)

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Capacity** | ~20–25 at peak (coaches + officials + clinic staff) |
| **Access** | Credentialed only — college coaches, credentialed HS coaches, tournament officials; lanyard at check-in |
| **Hours** | All event hours, both days |
| **Setup** | Comfortable seating; 2–3 work tables; charging stations; Wi-Fi; locking gear storage for officials |
| **AV** | TV(s) showing mat 1 + mat 2 live + bracket updates |
| **Hospitality** | Coffee/water all day · light continental Friday + Saturday AM · lunch service Saturday · snacks/light meals for officials between sessions |
| **Print station** | Brackets, athlete profile printouts, sharpies for note-taking |
| **Recruiting guides** | Sent to credentialed college coaches in advance; hard copies in lounge on event day |
| **Quiet area** | Curtained phone-booth section for calls |

**Sponsorship:** "Coaches & Officials Lounge presented by [Brand]" — legitimate **Champion-tier** benefit ($7,500–$10,000 starting range). Natural fits: regional restaurant, recovery brand, recruiting service. Adds revenue, defrays hospitality cost, gives sponsor face time with credentialed coaches.

**Unsponsored cost estimate (weekend):** **$1,500–$2,500**

| Line item | Estimate |
|-----------|----------|
| Catering | $800–$1,200 |
| AV rental (if not house-supplied) | $300–$500 |
| Credentialing materials + printing | $200–$400 |
| Lounge check-in staff | Volunteer |
