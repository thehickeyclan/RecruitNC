## 8a. Dedicated lounges

**Principle:** Two separate rooms — never combined. Coaches recruit; officials adjudicate. A shared lounge creates perceived conflict of interest.

**Hope Church angle:** Two of Hope Apex's five atrium rooms become the coaches and officials lounges. This is exactly the use case Lori identified as a venue strength — the five rooms are operationally essential, not optional overflow.

### College Coaches Lounge (larger room — recruiting made tangible)

| Item | Spec |
|------|------|
| **Capacity** | 15–20 coaches at peak |
| **Access** | Credentialed only — lanyard at check-in |
| **Hours** | All event hours, both days |
| **Setup** | Comfortable seating; 2–3 work tables; charging stations; Wi-Fi |
| **AV** | TV(s) showing mat 1 + mat 2 live + bracket updates |
| **Hospitality** | Coffee/water all day · light continental Friday + Saturday AM · lunch service Saturday · evening reception during finals |
| **Print station** | Brackets, athlete profile printouts, sharpies for note-taking |
| **Quiet area** | Curtained phone-booth section for calls |

**Sponsorship:** "College Coaches Lounge presented by [Brand]" — legitimate **Champion-tier** benefit ($7,500–$10,000 starting range). Natural fits: regional restaurant, recovery brand, recruiting service. Adds revenue, defrays hospitality cost, gives sponsor face time with college coaches and committed/committing families.

**Unsponsored cost estimate (weekend):** **$1,500–$2,500**

| Line item | Estimate |
|-----------|----------|
| Catering | $800–$1,200 |
| AV rental (if not house-supplied) | $300–$500 |
| Credentialing materials + printing | $200–$400 |
| Lounge check-in staff | Volunteer |

## Appendix A — Partnership pitch to Hope (Lori)

**Subject:** Tournament of Champions — atrium room plan + September 4–5 hold

Hi Lori,

We're finalizing operations for the NC United Tournament of Champions (September 4–5, 2026) and wanted to share how we plan to use Hope Apex's atrium spaces — specifically **two dedicated rooms** for our **College Coaches Lounge** and **Officials Lounge**.

This matches what you highlighted as a strength of the campus: flexible rooms that support a professional tournament, not just mats on the floor. We need **two of the five atrium rooms** reserved for the full weekend:

1. **College Coaches Lounge (larger)** — credentialed college staffs only; live mat feeds, work space, hospitality, and recruiting environment.
2. **Officials Lounge (smaller)** — separate from coaches and athletes; gear storage and rest between sessions.

We keep coaches and officials in **separate rooms** by design — coaches recruit, officials adjudicate, and combining them creates the wrong optics.

**Competition footprint:** two mats Friday–Saturday, single-mat finals Saturday evening; seating up to ~1,000; health-first concessions (no soda, chips, candy, or junk food venue-wide).

**Ask:** Confirm availability of two atrium rooms for Sept 4–5, 2026, plus any house AV in those rooms. Happy to walk the layout on a site visit.

Thank you —  
Matt Hickey / NC United
