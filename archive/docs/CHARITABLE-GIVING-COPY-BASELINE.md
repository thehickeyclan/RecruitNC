# Baseline language: NC United charitable gifts (archive & shared docs)

Use this block when rewriting **Spartan**, **fundraising hub**, **playbook excerpts**, emails, proposals, or any **archived markdown** that might be circulated. It mirrors production copy goals; it is **not tax advice**.

## Four pillars

1. **Gift recipient** — Donations support **NC United** and, where applicable, the **NC United Training Fund** (and other stated designations aligned with exempt purposes). Prefer naming the nonprofit entity plainly.

2. **Checkout / roster naming** — A **wrestler or designation named at checkout** is for **documentation and administration**, not a representation that funds are paid *to* that individual.

3. **Acknowledgments and receipts** — Describe **charitable acknowledgment** consistent with IRC documentation norms (organization name, EIN where appropriate, date, amount). Avoid promising outcomes the organization’s counsel has not approved.

4. **Deductibility** — Do **not** use blanket phrases like “tax-deductible” or “fully deductible” as universal facts. Prefer: whether a contribution is deductible **depends on the donor’s facts and circumstances**; donors should **consult a tax advisor or CPA**.

## EIN (reference only)

**EIN:** 99-3757238 — **NC United Wrestling** is described in public materials as a **501(c)(3)** organization. Organizational status is distinct from whether any specific donor may claim a deduction.

## Patterns to avoid in outward-facing prose

- “Every gift is tax-deductible.”
- “100% tax-deductible” without qualifiers.
- Implied IRS guarantee of deduction or reimbursement of amateur expenses.

## Short disclaimer snippet (adapt as needed)

> NC United Wrestling (EIN 99-3757238) provides charitable acknowledgments consistent with IRS documentation rules applicable to exempt organizations; whether you may deduct a contribution depends on your situation—please consult your tax advisor.
